    Top 10 Beach Wedding Resorts in India (2025 Edition)

    📅 December 2025 👤 Royal Tales Editorial Team ⏱ 7 min read ⭐ Trending

    Dreaming of a stunning beachside wedding in India? From Goa’s iconic beaches to Kerala’s serene backwaters, here are the top resorts that offer magical sunsets, premium hospitality, and picture-perfect spaces for your celebration.

    Beach weddings are always special — the sea breeze, sunset hues, barefoot mandap setups and soft waves as background music make the celebration feel cinematic and intimate. If you're searching for the perfect destination for your beach wedding in India, this guide will help you shortlist the best of the best.

    “Beach weddings are not just events — they’re an unforgettable experience crafted with nature, love, and emotion.”
    – Royal Tales, Wedding Specialists

    Here Are the Top 10 Beach Wedding Resorts in India

    1

    The Leela, Goa

    If you’re looking for luxury and privacy, The Leela Goa is one of the most iconic wedding destinations.
    Best For: Grand weddings with high-end décor
    Average Budget: ₹70 lakhs – ₹2.5 crores

    2

    Taj Exotica, Goa

    A stunning palm-fringed coastline makes this resort ideal for sunset pheras and welcome dinners.
    Best For: Traditional meets luxury
    Budget: ₹50 lakhs – ₹2 crores

    3

    ITC Grand Bharat, Kovalam

    Nestled beside Kerala’s quiet waters, this venue is perfect for elegant intimate weddings.
    Budget: ₹35 lakhs – ₹1.2 crores

    4

    Marriott Resort, Mahabalipuram

    Ideal for couples wanting a calming ocean setting close to Chennai — modern yet rooted in culture.

    5

    Radisson Blu Temple Bay, Tamil Nadu

    Known for huge open lawns and mandap structures — perfect for multi-event destination weddings.

    6

    The W Hotel, Goa

    For the modern boho couple — neon parties, pool weddings, beach cocktails and aesthetic corners.
    Instagram appeal: 10/10 🔥

    7

    Ramada Caravela Beach Resort, Goa

    Perfect for budget-conscious destination weddings without compromising the beach vibe.
    Budget Range: ₹20 lakhs – ₹70 lakhs

    8

    Barefoot Havelock, Andaman Islands

    A dreamy location where the ocean is turquoise and sunsets look unreal — ideal for intimate 50–120 guest weddings.

    9

    The Lalit Resort & Spa, Kerala

    Located at Bekal backwaters — a hybrid beach + lagoon wedding vibe few places can offer.

    10

    Novotel Candolim, Goa

    A great pick for modern lightweight décor weddings, pre-wedding pool parties and colorful haldi celebrations.

    Final Thoughts

    India has no shortage of stunning beach resorts for weddings — each offering its own vibe, budget and experience. Whether you want a grand luxurious celebration or an intimate beach ceremony with close family, there's a location perfectly made for your story.
