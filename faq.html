Frequently Asked Questions Questions

Can we choose our own vendors or do you only work with your partners?

We are flexible. While we have a strong network of trusted vendors, we are happy to work with your preferred vendors too. Our goal is to ensure quality and smooth coordination.

How early should we book The Royal Tales for our wedding?

Ideally, **3 to 12 months before your wedding date** to secure venues and vendors, especially in popular destinations like Jaipur, Udaipur, or Goa. However, we also handle last-minute weddings with efficient planning.

Do you also plan pre-wedding events like engagement or mehendi?

Absolutely 🎉 We manage **all functions** – engagement, haldi, mehendi, sangeet, wedding, and reception. Each event is given a unique theme and design for a complete royal experience.

Do you provide customised themes and décor for weddings?

Yes 🌸 Every couple has a unique story, and we design décor themes that reflect your personality, culture, and preferences. From royal palace settings to modern beachside vibes, our design team ensures a one-of-a-kind experience tailored just for you.

RoyalTales Faq's Faq's

Why should I hire The Royal Tales for my destination wedding?

At The Royal Tales, we specialise in creating stress-free and memorable destination weddings. Our team manages every detail – from décor, logistics, and entertainment to hospitality – so that you and your family can enjoy the celebration without worry.

Do you handle weddings only in India?

No, we manage both **domestic and international destination weddings**. In India, we cover Jaipur, Udaipur, Goa, Kerala, and more. Internationally, we work in popular wedding destinations like Dubai, Bali, and Thailand.

How much does a destination wedding cost with The Royal Tales?

Costs depend on location, number of guests, décor, and hospitality preferences. We customise packages to fit your vision and budget. A typical Indian destination wedding can start from ₹30 lakhs and go upwards.

Do you also manage guest travel and accommodation?

Yes ✅ Guest management is one of our strongest services. We arrange airport transfers, hotel check-ins, welcome kits, and even local sightseeing, ensuring guests feel comfortable throughout their stay.
