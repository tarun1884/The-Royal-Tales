    Destination Wedding in Udaipur Cost (2025 Guide)

    📅 December 2, 2025 👤 Vineet ⏱ 8 min read 👁 7.8K views

    Dreaming of a royal wedding in Udaipur? From palaces by the lake to luxury resorts, this guide breaks down the actual cost of a destination wedding in Udaipur in 2025 — with real ranges, hidden expenses, and smart saving tips.

    Why Udaipur Is India’s Royal Wedding Capital

    Udaipur is called the City of Lakes for a reason — shimmering waters, heritage palaces, sunsets that feel like cinema, and a charm you won’t find anywhere else. No surprise that couples from India and abroad choose Udaipur as their dream destination wedding spot.

    But the first big question is always the same: “Kitna kharcha hoga?” In this guide, we’ll walk you through realistic cost ranges for a destination wedding in Udaipur in 2025, so you can plan confidently with your family and your planner.

    “A royal Udaipur wedding can be tailored to your budget — it’s all about smart planning, the right venue, and the right team.”
    – The Royal Tales, Destination Wedding Specialists

    Average Cost of a Destination Wedding in Udaipur (2025)

    The cost of a Udaipur wedding depends on guest count, venue type, number of functions, décor level, and how “extra” you want to go. Here’s a quick overview for a 2–3 day celebration with 120–250 guests:

    Approximate Budget Ranges:

    • Elegant Intimate Wedding: ₹45 lakhs – ₹80 lakhs
    • Classic Royal Wedding: ₹80 lakhs – ₹1.8 crores
    • Ultra-Luxury Palace Wedding: ₹2 crores – ₹5 crores+

    These numbers include stay, venue rental, décor, basic entertainment, F&B, and logistics — but every wedding is different. The sections below break down where most of the money actually goes.

    Major Cost Components of a Udaipur Wedding

    1

    Venues & Room Nights

    Your biggest cost will be stay + venue.
    • Luxury Resorts & Palaces: Taj Lake Palace, Oberoi Udaivilas, The Leela, Fatehgarh.
    • Approx. Range (2 Nights, 120–200 Guests): ₹20 lakhs – ₹1.5 crores.
    • Pricing depends on season, weekday vs weekend, and exclusivity (full buyout vs partial booking).

    2

    Décor & Design

    Udaipur weddings are known for royal setups — think floral mandaps, palace-style entries, and lake-view dinner décor.
    • Simple Yet Elegant Décor: ₹8 lakhs – ₹15 lakhs.
    • Mid-Luxury Themed Décor: ₹15 lakhs – ₹35 lakhs.
    • High-End Custom Experiences: ₹35 lakhs – ₹80 lakhs+.
    Florals, stages, lounges, couple entries, welcome setups — all add up quickly.

    3

    Food & Beverage (F&B)

    Indian weddings are remembered for food. Guests will talk about your buffet long after the pheras!
    • Per Plate (Veg): ₹1,800 – ₹3,500 (approx.).
    • Per Plate (Non-Veg / Multi-Cuisine): ₹2,500 – ₹4,500.
    • Multiple Meals x Multiple Days: Breakfast, lunch, hi-tea, dinner, midnight snacks.
    • Range for 120–200 Guests: ₹8 lakhs – ₹35 lakhs.

    4

    Photography & Videography

    From cinematic films to candid photography, a good team captures your story forever.
    • Standard Good Team (2–3 Days): ₹2 lakhs – ₹6 lakhs.
    • Premium Teams / Well-Known Names: ₹6 lakhs – ₹20 lakhs+.
    Pre-wedding shoot, same-day edits, reels, drone shots can increase the budget.

    5

    Entertainment & Artists

    Sangeet, cocktail night, baraat, after-party – music sets the mood.
    • Local Bands / DJs: ₹50,000 – ₹2 lakhs per event.
    • Celebrity Singers / Known DJs: ₹5 lakhs – ₹50 lakhs+.
    • Add-ons: Dhol, folk dancers, fire acts, Sufi nights, live saxophonists.

    6

    Hospitality & Logistics

    This is where a good planner truly shines.
    • Airport & station transfers.
    • Check-in coordination, welcome desks, luggage handling.
    • Guest helpdesks, room drops, welcome hampers.
    Approx. Range: ₹3 lakhs – ₹20 lakhs depending on guest count & complexity.

    7

    Rituals, Styling & Extras

    • Pandit / priest, pheras setup, varmala stage. • Bridal & groom makeup, family styling teams. • Mehendi artists, dholwalas, horse/ghodi or vintage car for baraat.
    Approx. Range: ₹2 lakhs – ₹15 lakhs.

    Visual Inspiration: Udaipur Wedding Vibes

    A quick glimpse into the kind of ambience you can expect — lakeside mandaps, sunset sangeet setups, and palace courtyards glowing under fairy lights.

    Sample Budget Scenarios for Udaipur Weddings

    These are example figures to help you visualise what kind of experience fits which budget bracket. Real numbers will vary with your venue choices, dates, and customisation.

    1. Intimate Udaipur Wedding – 100 Guests

    • Boutique heritage hotel or smaller palace property
    • 2 days, 3–4 functions (Mehendi, Sangeet, Wedding, Reception)
    • Approx. Budget: ₹45 lakhs – ₹70 lakhs

    2. Classic Royal Wedding – 200–250 Guests

    • Well-known luxury resort / palace with lake or city views
    • 3 days, multi-event itinerary
    • Richer décor, live entertainment, premium F&B
    • Approx. Budget: ₹80 lakhs – ₹1.8 crores

    3. Ultra-Luxury Palace Takeover – 250+ Guests

    • Iconic palaces, near-full or full property buyout
    • Designer décor, celebrity artist, cinematic crew
    • Top-tier hospitality & personalised experiences
    • Approx. Budget: ₹2 crores – ₹5 crores+

    Hidden Costs Couples Often Forget

    Even the best spreadsheets miss a few things. Here are common “surprise” expenses you should keep in mind while planning your Udaipur destination wedding:

    Watch Out For:

    • Vendor travel & stay if your teams are from Mumbai/Delhi.
    • Extra décor for backup venues (in case of rain or weather issues).
    • Sound permissions, late-night music charges, and firework restrictions.
    • After-party setups, extended bar timing charges.
    • Last-minute room upgrades or extra nights for family/guests.
